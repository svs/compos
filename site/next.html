§ handledthe plumbing every harness needs

All the busywork, handled.

  • Chat UIStreaming replies, tool calls, components drawn inline.
  • ConcurrencyEvery agent, task and app runs in its own BEAM process. Nothing blocks.
  • OrchestrationSub-chats, workers and hand-offs, each with clean context.
  • RenderingBuffers, windows, listings, detail pages and components.
  • IPCMCP servers, the shell, HTTP and the browser, one call away.
  • EventsWebhooks, mail, SMS, WhatsApp and Slack wake the right agent.
  • SchedulingJobs that run at 7am, or when the work they wait on finishes.
  • PermissionsEvery call declares its effects. You decide what runs unattended.
  • ContextGroups hold the buffers, windows and chat of one task.
  • Memory and historyEvery save is versioned. Tasks carry their own record.
  • SecretsKeys stay in the vault, never in the prompt.
  • ModelsBring the subscriptions you already pay for.

§ contextgroups, not tabs

Ludicrously good context management.

Point the agent at where your attention is. Compos agents are more like copilots than bounty hunters.

Collect context into groups. One group per task: its buffers, its windows, its chat. Switch tasks and the whole room switches with you.

  • Your agent sees the windows you see. No pasting.
  • Give it a part of a file, not the whole drive.
  • A group keeps its layout. Come back tomorrow and it is all still there.
§ alivechange it while it runs

Effortless multitasking

Orchestrator agents run teams of workers, one per task, to keep context clean and costs low.

§ featureswhat changes for you
01 · primitives

Program in primitives designed for agentic execution.

Buffers, groups, effects, listeners, tasks. Small parts an agent can read, call and compose, and you can audit. Every automation is a few lines of Scheme you own.

02 · live

Fully live system. Connect anything.

Change it while it runs: no build, no restart, no deploy. Databases, inboxes, APIs, webhooks, MCP servers, the CLI: if you can reach it, an agent can wire it in.

03 · any harness

Drive any CLI harness. Even your own.

Claude Code, Codex, any ACP agent, or the one you wrote. Compos provides the application and UI layer; the harness brings the brain.

04 · ui

Ready-to-go UI capabilities.

Listings, detail pages, galleries, keys, components. Agents learn from the demo apps how to build beautiful UIs. Use it.

05 · proven

Battle tested in recruiting and coding.

Compos runs a recruiting business and builds itself, every day. The rough edges get found in real work, not in a demo.

06 · cost

No new AI bill.

Use the AI subscriptions you already pay for. No markup, no extra key, and your data stays yours.
