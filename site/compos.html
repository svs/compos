§ features what changes for you
01 · fits you

Work the way you think.

Your workspace fits your habits, not the other way round. Need a new tool? Ask for it and have it in minutes. When your work changes, your tools change with it.

02 · focus

Better answers, less explaining.

Your agent already knows what you are working on. Stop pasting, re-explaining and correcting. It stays on the task in front of you, and nothing from elsewhere leaks in.

03 · control

The right agent for every job.

A careful reviewer for one task, a fast drafter for the next. Keep the setups that work and use them again. You stay in charge of what an agent may do on its own.

04 · flow

Stop switching apps.

Mail, notes, reading and projects in one place, so you keep your train of thought. Find anything fast, connect ideas across sources, and keep moving.

05 · time

Get more done while you think.

Hand off the follow-ups, summaries and first drafts. Work comes back to you when it is ready, and nothing important goes out without your OK.

06 · cost

No new AI bill.

Use the AI subscriptions you already pay for. No markup, no extra key, and your data stays yours.

§ always on whatsapp · email · webhooks

It answers the world while you work.

A WhatsApp message, an email, an alert from the tools you already use. Your agents notice, take care of the routine, and bring you only what needs you. Nothing slips while you are heads down or away.
