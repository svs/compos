# Learn Compos

The canonical learn-by-doing tutorial is the tutorial package's
[`scheme/packages/tutorial/COMPOS.md`](../scheme/packages/tutorial/COMPOS.md).
In the tutorial, the lesson pane visits that file: edit a lesson there and
press `C-x C-s` to change the bundled tutorial. The slide follows as you type.

Inside Compos, press `C-h t` or choose **Start the tutorial** on the Welcome
page.
